Privacy Policy

Earn It Grow It Uploader. Last updated: 21 November 2026.

The Earn It Grow It Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Earn It Grow It YouTube channel to upload and schedule videos on that channel. This policy explains what the tool can access and what it does with it.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel owner uses the tool, with their own Google account and their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and no other people's accounts are connected to it.

What data the tool accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for these permissions on the owner's YouTube account through the YouTube API Services:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload the owner's videos and set their thumbnails.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl: to add captions, set titles, descriptions and publish times, and read back the status of the owner's own uploads.

The tool only works with videos on the owner's own channel. It does not access viewer data, comments of other users, contacts, email, or any other Google services.

How data is used and stored

  • Video files, thumbnails, captions and metadata are sent directly to YouTube from the private environment where the owner runs the tool.
  • The OAuth access credentials are kept only in the owner's own private environment settings. They are not sent to any other person or third-party server.
  • The tool writes the resulting YouTube video IDs and statuses into the owner's own private schedule file.
  • The tool does not run analytics, tracking or advertising, and stores nothing on third-party servers.

Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone. Data obtained through the YouTube API Services is used only to perform the uploads and scheduling the owner asks for.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page. After access is revoked the tool can no longer reach the YouTube account. Deleting the stored credentials from the environment settings removes them completely.

YouTube and Google terms

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
